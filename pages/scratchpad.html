01 · Mental model

An evidence machine, not an algorithm in a box.

VHF separates knowledge, research declarations, calculations, and promotion authority. A good idea cannot bypass timing rules, data lineage, risk controls, reproducible replay, or human review simply because a backtest looks attractive.

01 / RAW

Source

Immutable books, papers, and later approved data bytes. Originals are never silently rewritten.

02 / WIKI

Concept

Compiled definitions, assumptions, contradictions, citations, and implementation lenses.

03 / SPEC

Strategy

A preregistered signal, universe, timing, benchmark, costs, trial family, and evaluation plan.

04 / CODE

Implementation

Typed, versioned, defensive contracts with explicit timestamps, hashes, and failure modes.

05 / PROOF

Evaluation

Replayable artifacts, reconciled results, out-of-sample tests, diagnostics, and limitations.

06 / GATE

Promotion

A future human-governed decision. Evidence stages cannot promote themselves.

Truth plane

Time is part of every value.

Security identity, effective date, availability time, revision, and immutable source identity travel with data. “What was knowable at the decision close?” is enforced rather than assumed.

Decision plane

Research intent is frozen first.

Run specifications bind the universe, signal, schedule, trial family, costs, benchmark, and evaluation protocol before results can tempt the system into changing the question.

Control plane

Failure is named and replayable.

Risk rejection, stale evidence, infeasibility, incomplete history, lifecycle conflicts, and unresolved accounting states fail closed with stable reasons instead of producing a convenient estimate.

02 · Build log

What has been built, phase by phase.

Phases 1–5 are complete within their declared synthetic boundaries. Phase 6 has complete provider-neutral contracts and fixtures, but its title—“first historical momentum experiment”—is intentionally not yet satisfied.

01

Frozen synthetic vertical slice

Hardened a complete daily-equity path from synthetic point-in-time prices through quality momentum, factor risk, factor-neutral targets, named risk decisions, next-open fills, and reconciled accounting. Public records validate inputs, copy mutable data, and serialize deterministically.

  • 650-session, 12-asset deterministic fixture.
  • Beginner walkthrough traces one rebalance end to end.
  • Timing, QLIKE labels, PSD bounds, drawdown, RSI, and configuration defects fixed.
  • Full-result digest frozen as the non-regression anchor.
Complete
02

Orchestration, events, artifacts, and replay

Separated strategy, risk model, portfolio policy, risk policy, execution venue, and event sink behind injected interfaces. A mandatory immutable run specification and canonical manifest now determine identities and block leakage.

  • Hash-chained, append-only events with UTC and exchange-local time.
  • Deterministic run, decision, proposal, fill, state, and artifact IDs.
  • Exactly five persisted files with SHA-256 inventory.
  • Component-free replay reconstructs cash, holdings, P&L, costs, and decisions.
Complete
03

Point-in-time US-equity data foundation

Added a provider-neutral, long-form data contract built around permanent identities, exact availability cutoffs, contiguous revisions, immutable source files, and deterministic materialization. It is ready to assess a real provider, not to pretend one already exists.

  • Twelve exact source tables cover identity, sessions, prices, actions, membership, liquidity, benchmarks, and factors.
  • Raw prices and total-return levels remain separate by design.
  • Raw-only execution-open snapshots prevent adjusted-price fills.
  • Provider scorecard exists; every provider result remains unassessed.
Foundation complete
04

Execution, accounting, and operational risk

Added a separate operational journal for next-regular-open routing, whole-lot partial fills, transaction-cost assumptions, immutable lifecycle evidence, corporate actions, delistings, borrow, and persistent halts.

  • Atomic batch risk with universal blockers, limits, and warnings.
  • Signed cash, position, realized/unrealized P&L, and cost ledgers.
  • Exact short-exposure intervals preserve financing history.
  • Idempotent operational events replay without strategy or venue components.
Complete
05

Dated risk models and portfolio construction

Added decision-dated beta, sector, and size exposures; five covariance candidates; strict later-window comparison; explicit alpha sizing; and a transparent one-dimensional convex-path optimizer with complete postchecks.

  • Fundamental ridge, sample, EWMA, shrinkage, and rolling PCA candidates.
  • PSD, label, vintage, loading, calibration, and turnover diagnostics.
  • Tolerance-neutral and bounded-factor portfolio declarations.
  • Costs and uncertainty can only reduce—not enlarge—the fixed-path trade.
Complete
06

Momentum experiment preregistration and synthetic scaffold

Encoded the future historical experiment before choosing a data source: exact universe rules, signal definitions, decision calendars, candidate audits, portfolio seeds, trial registry, robustness schedules, and evaluation protocol. No performance or winner field exists in this boundary.

  • Exact 12-minus-2 total-return momentum with path-quality and signed-MAX evidence.
  • Top-50 long-only seed and membership-only long/short seed under 2% name caps.
  • Ten-trial family plus preregistered block/Rademacher maximum-Sharpe procedure.
  • Five integrated acceptance scenarios; provider and history remain blocked.

05 · Phase 6 gate

The experiment is specified. The experiment is not run.

This distinction is the most important current project fact. The scaffold can audit approved point-in-time inputs and produce synthetic formation evidence; it cannot manufacture the licensed historical data, folds, costs, borrow, capacity, or human decision required for promotion.

Built and synthetic-tested

  • USD ordinary common shares on XNYS, XNAS, or XASE.
  • NYSE 40th-percentile market-cap breakpoint and positive 60-session ADV floor.
  • Thirteen declared month ends for exact 12-minus-2 total-return momentum.
  • Information discreteness from 252 daily returns and signed skipped-month MAX.
  • Source-faithful top-50 long-only seed under an exact 2% name cap.
  • Long/short decile membership seed, with final Phase 5 sizing and neutralization blocked.
  • Monthly and three robustness rebalance schedules.
  • Ten-trial family, four benchmarks, five covariance candidates, and future test protocol.
  • Hash-bound decision-calendar prefixes and raw-next-open route declaration.

Missing—and therefore blocked

  • No evaluated or selected point-in-time provider.
  • No approved license, budget, credentials, dependency, storage, or export policy.
  • No immutable provider source bytes or historical materialization.
  • No materialized expanding folds and no locked final three-year holdout.
  • No historical gross/net returns, Sharpe ratio, drawdown, turnover, costs, capacity, or attribution.
  • No multiple-testing statistic and no winning or canonical trial.
  • No verified borrow availability or short cost for short export.
  • No paper proposal export, broker adapter, broker connection, or live evidence.

07 · Owner controls

What the project expects from Boz.

The system is intentionally waiting at a human decision boundary. Boz does not need to choose a provider immediately; he needs to decide whether provider assessment should begin and define the constraints under which it may proceed.

Owner decision queue

Each approval is discrete. Saying yes to one item does not authorize the next.

HUMAN_APPROVAL_REQUIRED
01

Authorize provider evaluation—or keep the gate closed.

A useful next instruction is simply one of: “evaluate provider options against the scorecard” or “leave the provider gate closed.” Evaluation is research only; it is not permission to buy, install, authenticate, download, or select.

02

Set provider constraints before comparison.

For a meaningful scorecard, specify or approve:

  • budget ceiling and acceptable billing model;
  • research-use, storage, export, and retention requirements;
  • minimum date range and coverage quality;
  • whether credentials and a new pinned dependency are acceptable.
03

Approve a provider and ingestion plan separately.

After reviewing a completed scorecard, explicitly approve—or reject—the provider, license/cost, immutable storage location, credential path, adapter dependency, and bounded initial download. No choice should be inferred from technical convenience.

04

Authorize historical execution, then review—not rubber-stamp—the result.

Approve fold materialization and holdout locking before the run. Later, review walk-forward, holdout, factor, drawdown, cost, capacity, attribution, and multiple-testing evidence. The valid outcome may be to reject every trial.

Nothing else is silently expected. Broker credentials, IBKR changes, paper orders, live orders, data purchases, public code publication, dependency changes, and evidence-stage promotion remain outside the current authority unless Boz asks for them explicitly.
