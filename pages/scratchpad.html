01 · Core mental model

One signal, several layers of uncertainty.

The pilot does not ask only “what did the selected portfolio return?” It asks what was selected, when it could first trade, whether entry and exit prices exist, which terminal outcomes are documented, how trading assumptions change the arithmetic, whether the book fits a liquidity guardrail, and whether factor-neutral targets can even be formed.

01 · Inputs

Bound archives

Current-vintage metadata, prices, capitalization, and action classifications.

02 · Screen

Eligible universe

Common shares, history, size, liquidity, and complete signal evidence.

03 · Rank

Momentum quality

12-minus-2 momentum, path discreteness, and signed recent MAX.

04 · Target

Top 50

Two percent per name; any unavailable entry remains cash.

05 · Route

Next open

Decision information dated T first affects the next reviewed session.

06 · Bound

Three exit paths

Total loss, documented lower recovery, and full-principal sensitivity.

07 · Diagnose

Compare and gate

Benchmarks, turnover, costs, capacity, factors, and promotion blockers.

What “current-vintage” means

Today’s adjusted history can contain later knowledge.

The calculation uses a bound five-year snapshot and respects a one-session trading lag, but it cannot reconstruct every historical revision or classification as it appeared at each decision date.

How to read terminal paths

Bounds are not point estimates.

Total loss is the rigorous nonnegative-recovery floor. The reviewed path is a tighter gross lower bound. Full-principal recovery is only a sensitivity and is not an upper bound.

How to read factor control

A feasible target is not a realized portfolio.

The solver can construct neutral weights at every decision, but missing long/short routes, unknown short terminal value, borrow, financing, and state propagation prevent a performance calculation.

Terminology map

Decision T

Economic date, not instant availability

Information dated to session T can first influence the next reviewed regular-session open.

Adjusted / raw route

Research return versus execution diagnostic

Adjusted opens carry total-return research economics; reconstructed raw opens remain routing diagnostics, not executable quotes.

Missing entry

Uninvested weight stays cash

The intended allocation earns zero for that holding period and is never redistributed to names that happened to have data.

One-way turnover

Half the risky-weight L1 change

Cash reconciles the state but is excluded from the sum so deployment is not counted twice.

Capacity limit

The first 5%-ADV bottleneck

A rounded constant-AUM sensitivity determined by the most restrictive private trade leg—not an executable fund size.

Formal evidence stage

Still synthetic-integrated

The provider-backed diagnostic is a separate smoke pilot and does not promote the canonical research system by itself.

02 · Fixed algorithm

The complete monthly calculation.

The formulas below are the stable public-safe specification that the v8 aggregate implements. They expose units, timing, signs, and failure rules while withholding licensed rows and identities.

01

Reconstruct research and liquidity routes

A provider-supplied split-adjusted close acts as the denominator that maps the reported open into adjusted and raw domains. Raw notional then supports a complete trailing liquidity filter.

Implementation lens

Require positive finite price levels, permit zero but not negative volume/notional, and never bridge a missing day.

adjusted_open = reported_open × total_return_close / split_adjusted_close
raw_open      = reported_open × raw_close / split_adjusted_close
raw_volume    = reported_volume × raw_close / split_adjusted_close
raw_notional  = raw_close × raw_volume

ADV60 = mean(raw_notional over 60 complete sessions)
Adjusted opens measure the research path. Raw opens and notional remain routing and liquidity diagnostics.
02

Build the eligible universe at each decision

Start with active USD domestic common shares, require thirteen complete observed month ends and positive capitalization, then form a linear 40th-percentile size breakpoint over that complete reference set. Historical primary-venue evidence is unavailable, so this is an all-eligible substitute rather than the canonical NYSE breakpoint.

Fail-closed rule

Incomplete 60-session ADV or signal history excludes the name; it is not imputed.

b_t = linear_quantile_40%(market_cap_i,t in complete reference set)

eligible_i,t = active_i,t
            ∧ 13_complete_month_ends_i,t
            ∧ market_cap_i,t > b_t
            ∧ complete(ADV60_i,t)
            ∧ ADV60_i,t ≥ $1,000,000
            ∧ complete(signal_history_i,t)
03

Measure 12-minus-2 momentum and path quality

Momentum ends one month before the decision, deliberately skipping the recent month. Information discreteness measures whether the formation path arrived through many small moves or fewer jumps. Zero-return days stay in the 252-day denominator. MAX is signed, never converted into an absolute jump.

How it connects

Raw momentum chooses the tail; FIP and MAX order names only inside that high-momentum tail.

M_i,t  = P_TR(i, formation_end) / P_TR(i, formation_start) − 1

ID_i,t = sign(M_i,t) × (n_negative / 252 − n_positive / 252)
MAX_i,t = max(signed daily return in the skipped recent month)

n_tail = max(1, ceil(0.10 × N_eligible))
q_ID   = percentile_rank(−ID) within the high-momentum tail
q_MAX  = percentile_rank(−MAX) within the high-momentum tail
quality = 0.5 × q_ID + 0.5 × q_MAX
04

Select, size, and route the monthly holding

Sort the top momentum decile by quality, use a stable identity only as a deterministic tie-break, and select at most fifty names. Each selected name receives 2%; any unfilled allocation stays cash.

Timing boundary

Decision T enters at the next reviewed open and exits at the next monthly rebalance’s reviewed open.

S_t = first min(50, n_tail) names by descending quality
w_i,t = 0.02 if i ∈ S_t, otherwise 0
w_cash,t = 1 − Σ_i w_i,t

r_i,t = adjusted_open_i,exit / adjusted_open_i,entry − 1
r_portfolio,t = Σ_i invested_weight_i,t × r_i,t

missing entry → allocation remains cash
05

Carry three terminal-recovery paths

A missing exit is never silently treated as a normal zero. The total-loss path is the unconditional floor. Reviewed positive cash can tighten that floor only when every omitted leg was checked as additive and nonnegative. Complete evidence can establish an exact outcome; unresolved cases stay unresolved.

Evidence boundary

Full-principal recovery is a sensitivity, not an upper bound; acquisition value can exceed entry value.

anchor = last valid target raw+adjusted open on/before effectiveness and before exit
adjustment_scale = adjusted_open_at_anchor / raw_open_at_anchor
adjusted_cash    = reviewed_cash_per_unit × adjustment_scale
successor_start  = explicit first-held session for the supported successor
successor_raw_exit = fixed_shares × raw_start × adjusted_exit / adjusted_start
adjusted_stock   = successor_raw_exit × adjustment_scale
documented_multiple = reviewed_adjusted_value_at_exit / adjusted_entry
r_documented    = documented_multiple − 1

unresolved total-loss path       → r = −1
unresolved full-principal path   → r = 0
reviewed documented-lower path   → substitute only admitted lower return
06

Chain monthly metrics without hiding path semantics

Metrics are calculated from one contiguous monthly series. Sharpe uses a zero reference rate and sample standard deviation. Drawdown begins from an explicit wealth origin of one.

Reading note

Changing a terminal path changes the entire chained wealth path; the columns are scenarios, not confidence intervals.

W_T       = Π_t (1 + r_t)
cumulative = W_T − 1
annualized = W_T^(12 / T) − 1
volatility = sample_sd(r_t) × √12
Sharpe_0   = mean(r_t) / sample_sd(r_t) × √12
drawdown_t = W_t / max_{s≤t}(W_s) − 1
MDD        = min_t(drawdown_t)

06 · Evidence boundaries

What the pilot can and cannot support.

The most useful result is not a single return number. It is a map of which conclusions survive each evidence limitation and which attractive interpretations still fail closed.

Data vintage

Bound and reproducible locally

Source content is checked before calculation and again after all streaming passes, but current-vintage backward adjustments do not reconstruct historical correction vintages or open the canonical provider gate.

Calendar

Reviewed across maximum history

The exact 7,207-session XNYS axis is pinned from 1997 through the source end, including regular observances and extraordinary closures. Calendar authority does not imply price or metadata authority.

Terminal evidence

Classification is not valuation

All selected gaps are event-associated, yet exact terminal value remains incomplete for three selected exits and most benchmark exits.

Turnover and cost

Arithmetic, not calibration

Turnover follows the established accounting convention. Spread, commission, and impact inputs remain illustrative and do not constitute net performance.

Factor control

Target feasibility only

Static sector metadata and incomplete long/short routes prevent historical neutral performance, attribution, or borrow-aware implementation.
